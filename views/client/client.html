Services

DATA ACQUISITION

DATA COLLECTION

Text collection, labelling, speech collection (utterance), sentiment analysis: our services include audio, music, and video categorization, classification, labelling, tagging, and broadcast subtitles.

DATA ANNOTATION

In the age of AI, data is the fuel for all analytic and machine learning. Lifewood provides high-quality annotation services for a wide range of mediums including text, image, audio, and video for both computer vision and natural language processing.

DATA CURATION

DATA VALIDATION

The goal is to create data that is consistent, accurate, and complete, preventing data loss or errors in transfer, code, or configuration. We verify that data conforms to predefined standards, rules, or constraints, ensuring the information is trustworthy and fit for its intended purpose.

Lifewood Transformation

Data transformation is the process of converting data from one format or structure to another that better suits your needs.

The single intention is to make it more usable. Once it’s been transformed you can do more things efficiently and reliably, like transforming data analytics to gain business intelligence or to check something practical, like underground cable installations over the past year.

And of course, you can apply mathematical functions for machine learning or data science purposes. The scale, automation and trust required by modern corporations can only be achieved with AI/ML capabilities. This requires data sifted and transformed to deliver a reliable source that:

  • Facilitates cost-efficient storage
  • Improves ease of analysis
  • Prioritises resources
  • Limits compliance risks
